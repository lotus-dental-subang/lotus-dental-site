Teeth Whitening Price in Malaysia

Teeth whitening has been a common practice for ages. It is a dentistry aesthetic procedure used to whiten discoloured teeth. As it is affordable, painless, effective, and produces results right away, teeth whitening is a frequent and popular aesthetic dental procedure.

The best approach to enhance your appearance and increase your confidence is by having your teeth whitened. It can be done at home as well as at our clinic, Lotus Dental!

What is Teeth Whitening?

Teeth whitening is the procedure whereby stains and discoloration are broken down using oxidizing agents. However, it is not a one-time procedure. You’ll have to do it on a frequent basis to keep your teeth white.

A variety of things can cause discolouration in our teeth. Some of the major culprits are:

  • Coffee, tea, and soft drinks
  • Poor dental hygiene
  • Ageing
  • Certain medications
  • Smoking

Can Teeth Whitening Hurt Your Teeth?

According to numerous research, teeth whitening is both safe and effective. However, there are some minor side effects which include:

  • Gum Irritation

The most common active chemicals in teeth whitening treatments are hydrogen peroxide and carbamide peroxide, which can cause irritation if they come into contact with your gums. Don’t worry – the dentist at Lotus Dental takes extra care to protect your gum tissue.

  • Tooth Sensitivity

This is related to peroxide-induced pulp inflammation during the treatment. Many things can influence the level of sensitivity, but don’t be overly worried because it may resolve on its own!

Types of Teeth Whitening

There are 5 types of teeth-whitening treatments that can help your smile appear brighter and give you a boost of self-confidence. The methods are:

  • White Toothpaste

Today’s toothpaste contains small levels of peroxide, which can help eliminate surface stains and make teeth appear whiter. However, the effects of simply using toothpaste are usually very mild.

  • Whitening Strips

These can be described as flexible, thin strips of plastic that have a peroxide-based bleaching agent on them. Your tooth enamel reacts with the peroxide in the whitening agent, lightening it. Sometimes, this process is referred to as “oxidation.”

  • Customised Tray Bleaching

As part of the manufacturing process for the moulds for your whitening trays, your dentist will take impressions of your teeth. You’d have to wear the trays for up to 1.5 hours per day for 1-2 weeks while using a bleaching solution inside of them. The colour of your teeth will noticeably improve in that period.

  • Whitening Pen

A teeth-whitening pen works as much as it sounds. Each pen contains a whitening mixture (the specifics will depend on the manufacturer) that may be used to brighten your teeth while you’re on the go.

  • In-Office Teeth Whitening

In-office whitening of teeth produces fast results. Your teeth’s whiteness will probably noticeably improve after just one treatment.

Benefits of Teeth Whitening Treatment at Lotus Dental

Teeth whitening treatment at Lotus Dental is performed by a dental professional and is usually completed in just one visit. After applying a bleaching agent to the teeth, a specific light or laser is used to activate the substance, which allows for a quicker whitening process. A couple of cycles may be necessary to achieve the optimum results.

The number of cycles refers to the period of time the whitening gel is applied to the teeth. Although the results are usually instantaneous, treatment can be costly. It can be a great alternative for those who want to see benefits right away.

How Long Will Teeth Whitening Last?

According to estimates, the benefits of whitening can last between 8 months to 1 year. This, however, will differ from person to person. If you smoke, consume stain-causing foods or beverages, or both, the impact is less likely to stay as long.

Teeth Whitening Price in Malaysia

Depending on the method you choose, the teeth whitening cost in Malaysia may vary. There are many choices available and you are sure to find one that fits your budget.

Most at-home teeth whitening kits cost less than RM1000, making them a cost-effective solution. They are also popular since they are readily available and simple to use, in addition to being reasonably priced.

The teeth whitening price in Malaysia for full teeth whitening procedures can be anywhere from RM800 and RM2000 (prices vary among dentists and cities). Even though it costs more, a lot of individuals prefer in-office teeth whitening because they believe the results are better.
