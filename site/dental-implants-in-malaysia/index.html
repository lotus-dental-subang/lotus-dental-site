Dental Implants in Malaysia (2023)

By Lotus Dental Clinic, SS15 Subang Jaya · 25 July 2023

This guide was written in 2023. Prices may have changed. WhatsApp us for today’s price.

Do you want to improve or restore your smile with dental implants? It’s a major decision for anyone, both in terms of expense and surgery, so we’ve put together this complete information so you can learn everything about dental implants in Malaysia!

What are Dental Implants?

Dental implants are the most common treatment for tooth loss. Lack of or poor dental hygiene, plaque and tartar buildup, tooth decay, and gum disease are just a few of the many causes of tooth loss. Dental implants are made up of an artificial tooth root that is surgically inserted into the jawbone to provide a stable, long-lasting basis for a new tooth. Since it replaces both the tooth and the root, an implant is different from other ways of replacing teeth.

If you lose a tooth and don’t replace it, it will lead to a chain reaction of alterations. One small adjustment will lead to more and more, changing the way your teeth and mouth function as a whole. Shifts in your bite can result in issues with your dental occlusion, which can result in a number of issues that have an impact on your life outside of your mouth.

When are Dental Implants Necessary?

When you have lost teeth but are unable or unwilling to acquire dentures, bridges, or crowns, dental implants are what you need. As the effects of removing or losing a tooth and doing nothing might worsen over time, it is crucial to replace a missing tooth.

Dental implants play a significant part in the overall operation of your mouth in addition to their many aesthetic benefits. Dental implants maintain a healthy bite, holding every tooth firmly in place while supporting a strong jawline and halting bone loss. The jawbone may start to degenerate after a tooth extraction or loss if it is not replaced. This occurs because the pressure and stimulus of teeth grinding are what initially protect the jawbone.

Dental implants could seem necessary from a cosmetic standpoint as well. Dental implants can help you if you’re self-conscious about the way your smile looks and feels. Your ability to eat comfortably, smile and laugh without experiencing pain, stress, or holding back is crucial and will have a significant impact on the quality of your life as a whole.

Types of Dental Implants in Malaysia

Titanium is the most popular dental implant in Malaysia.

Titanium dental implants are commonly used to repair teeth that are damaged or missing. They are most effective in candidates who are in generally good health. Titanium implants, which have been used since the 1960s, are the most popular form of implant due to their endurance and usefulness. Titanium dental implants are intended to last a lifetime. However, after a few years, you might need to replace them.

Benefits of Dental Implants in Malaysia

Dental implants have a number of benefits. They function just like your natural teeth and can significantly boost your overall self-confidence. Besides that, they mimic your natural teeth in appearance and feel, as well as increase your self-esteem.

Other than that, they are also practical, long-lasting, and able to improve your overall dental health. Moreover, they support your ability to speak, make you feel comfortable, and chew even more easily. In contrast to alternatives like dentures, dental implants do not need to be taken out and you can brush, floss, eat, and even sleep with them in.

Dental implants in Malaysia also cost up to 50% less when compared to Australia, so, doing dental implants in Malaysia is a smart move for those who wish to do so.

Cost of Dental Implants in Malaysia

The cost of dental implants in Malaysia is considered affordable when compared to dental implants in other countries. It is determined by the type of implant chosen and the number of implants required.

Dental implants in Malaysia cost between RM5000 and RM10000 per implant, with the average cost being RM8000.

In the United States and the United Kingdom, you should expect to pay between RM12000 and RM20000 per implant which gives the same implant quality as Malaysia’s.
