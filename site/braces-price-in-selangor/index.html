The most effective and precise method of moving teeth would be to use braces. It consists of wires that slot into brackets to straighten and move the teeth, metal bands that fit around the back molar teeth to keep the braces on, and brackets that are affixed to the teeth’s surface.

Braces are most commonly utilised throughout youth, however, adults are increasingly getting corrective dental braces later in life.

Some health and dental insurance policies cover braces for dependent children. The quantity of coverage you receive, however, will be determined by the insurance company and the cost of the services recommended by your dentist.

What are Braces?

Dental braces are devices that are used to straighten crowded or crooked teeth, as well as a misaligned jaw (malocclusion). They are constructed of metal or ceramic, wires, and a bonding substance that is used to adhere them to your teeth. A doctor who specialises in this type of equipment and medical care for misaligned teeth is known as a dentist.

Brace success rates vary depending on your age when treatment begins and your treatment goals. Braces are normally highly effective for those who use them; nevertheless, their effectiveness is dependent on the individual and their ability to meticulously follow their dentist’s advice.

Common Types of Braces in Malaysia and The Prices

Metal Braces

Source: grantortho.com

Metal braces are applied by glueing metal brackets to each of your individual teeth and then running a metal archwire across them to press your teeth into their right places. Then, the colourful elastic bands will hold the archwire in place.

Ceramic Braces

Source: arizonabraces.com

Ceramic or clear braces are orthodontic devices used to straighten teeth and correct bite issues. They are made out of rubber bands and clear or tooth-coloured wires that are affixed to the surface of your teeth, giving them more discretion.

The only difference between the two orthodontic appliances is that one is made of metal and the other is made of ceramic.

Braces prices in Selangor for ceramic braces start at RM7000, depending on your dental health. For a thorough consultation with one of our dentists, please contact us here!

Invisalign

Source: batterseasmiles.co.uk

Invisalign is an orthodontic procedure that uses clear aligners instead of metal braces to straighten teeth. Invisalign is a set of custom-made clear braces that cover your teeth and gradually pull them into the appropriate position. They are less obvious than typical metal braces since they are clear and can be taken on and off.

Why Do You Need Braces?

Do you smile without your teeth? Do you bring your hand over your mouth when you’re laughing? Even though many people have crooked teeth, if your teeth are causing you discomfort or insecurity, you should visit us at Lotus Dental.

Here are some reasons why you need braces:

Crowding

One of the most common reasons for getting braces is this. If it appears that there isn’t enough room in your mouth for all of your teeth, you may have a crowding problem that causes your teeth to overlap. Correcting crowded teeth can significantly improve the appearance of your smile.

Spacing

Wide gaps between your teeth, when you smile, could be the result of missing teeth or teeth that are proportionately smaller than the rest of your mouth.

It could appear only cosmetic to fill up the large gaps in your grin. However, you can also use it to protect your oral health. Wide gaps between your teeth can allow food to become lodged and build up on your gums, leading to foul breath, cavities, and other dental problems.

Overbite

If your bottom teeth totally disappear when you bite or occasionally bite into the roof of your mouth, you may have a severe overbite. An extreme overbite might harm your front teeth and gum tissue if it is not treated.

Underbite

When you smile, your lower teeth may cover your top front teeth, which indicates that you may have an underbite that our dentists may fix. When eating or speaking, underbites can be especially damaging and raise your risk of oral injury.

Open Bite

You may have an open bite if there is a visible space between your upper and lower teeth. Patients who have open bites frequently have trouble speaking or eating properly. Additionally, they could have uncomfortable tendencies like tongue thrusting.
