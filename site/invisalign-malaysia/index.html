Invisalign Malaysia: The Ultimate Guide (2023)

What is Invisalign?

Invisalign is an invisible orthodontics procedure which is an invisible alternative to braces. Invisalign is free of metal wires and is nearly unnoticeable when worn. Customised trays are made especially for your teeth, and you’ll wear a new tray every several weeks in order to gradually straighten your teeth.

Depending on how complex the dental situation is, Invisalign treatment can take anywhere from 6 to 24 months.

Invisalign Clear Aligners VS Conventional Braces

In many circumstances, though, both alternatives are accessible, allowing you to choose what you want and what best matches your lifestyle.

Conventional braces are metal or porcelain devices that are affixed to your teeth. The wires of metal and rubber bands are then utilised to apply force to the teeth, allowing them to move into the proper position.

On the other hand, Invisalign is made out of plastic and is tailored to your specific teeth, almost like invisible braces in Malaysia. Each aligner gradually brings the teeth closer to their final place. To help the aligner grab the tooth and move it correctly, little bumps of composite resin (tooth-coloured filling material) are applied to specific teeth.

Pros of Invisalign Clear Aligners

  • Less Visible

They are less obvious than braces since they are transparent and custom-made to suit your teeth.

  • More Comfortable

Since Invisalign is removable, you’ll be able to eat peacefully without it disrupting the comfort of you eating your favourite food. Furthermore, since the Invisalign clear aligner is smoother than metal or porcelain braces, the lips and cheeks are less inflamed. This may be especially important for persons who participate in contact sports.

  • Hygiene

As they are detachable, you’re free to brush and floss as usual. This is significant since many people find it difficult to clean their teeth while wearing braces.

  • More Convenient

The actual visits to the practice are usually considerably simpler and, in certain situations, can be fewer overall since everything is already pre-planned by your dentist. This might be a huge benefit for anyone who has a hectic schedule.

Cons of Invisalign Clear Aligners

  • Cost

In rare circumstances, Invisalign Malaysia’s cost might be more than braces. Before you make a decision, talk to your dentist about the costs of your options.

  • Compliance

For an Invisalign to work, they must be worn for 22 hours every day. This implies that they are only taken out for cleaning and eating. Compliance produces outcomes so you have to be disciplined!

Pros of Conventional Braces

  • All Cases Are Covered

Whereas Invisalign can treat the majority of instances, braces can treat all cases. As a result, braces are always an option.

  • Various Braces

Braces can be of many metal types, with or without colouring. There are also porcelain braces, which are far less noticeable than traditional metal braces.

  • Compliance

Since the braces are attached to your teeth, you won’t ever lose or misplace them. This can be beneficial for certain patients who are not driven to wear Invisalign properly.

Cons of Conventional Braces

  • Hygiene

If teeth are not cleaned properly, serious harm can occur. This could result in making the choice to remove your braces before achieving the best possible end result.

  • Comfort

You won’t be able to enjoy all of your favourite foods while wearing braces because they may cause damage (particularly hard foods). Braces are also more prone to cause discomfort because they might rub against the cheeks and lips. Some patients are more affected than others, and it is often a factor for those who engage in sports activities.

  • Appearance

Braces are more apparent than Invisalign. Depending on their work, hobbies, and personality, this is significant for some patients.

  • Appointment

Braces appointments are typically longer and more intensive, with continuous adjustments performed in the chair. There may also be a need for more regular visits.

How To Know If Invisalign Is For Me?

You’ll know that Invisalign is for you when you tick off all of these factors:

  • The Severity Of Your Dental Problem

Invisalign can help with almost any condition, and while it is ideal for treating a wide range of ailments such as gapped teeth, crowded teeth, misaligned or crooked teeth and overcrowding.

Invisalign has a difficult time resolving serious conditions such as severe over/underbites or crossbites. Patients with certain dental problems may require more traditional treatments that are more suitable.

  • Age Factor

For patients who do not yet have all of their adult teeth, dentists generally do not advise teeth straightening. This is due to the difficulty in predicting how children’s permanent teeth will develop. For Invisalign to work, a patient’s adult teeth must be all present. In some cases, young adults may still have one or two baby teeth. If so, additional orthodontic work might be required before starting the Invisalign process.

  • Ready for Commitment

Invisalign is only offered to people above a specific age since patients must be committed to wearing them in order to see the results. As the Invisalign trays are removable, users can remove them whenever they want.

Invisalign works best when they are worn for a specific number of hours each day which typically entails wearing them continuously for at least 22 hours and taking them out just to eat or brush your teeth.

  • Great Oral Hygiene

To use Invisalign trays, your dental hygiene must be well-maintained. In order to prevent infections or other issues from arising, patients must not just have good oral hygiene but also be able to give adequate attention to the trays.

The trays must be regularly cleaned by the patients. Otherwise, the tray could need to be replaced due to damage, which would incur additional costs. Before prescribing the procedure, the dentist will make sure the patient has excellent oral hygiene.
