Filling Teeth Price in Malaysia (2023)

By Lotus Dental Clinic, SS15 Subang Jaya · 25 July 2023

This guide was written in 2023. Prices may have changed. WhatsApp us for today’s price.

Teeth fillings, also known as dental fillings, are used to repair natural tooth structure that has been fractured, chipped, or destroyed as a result of tooth decay. If you require a filling, your dentist might employ a variety of filling materials. Dental fillings will restore your tooth’s appearance and function.

What is Teeth Filling?

Teeth filling is used to heal teeth that have cavities due to oral acidity. Cement or asphalt fillings are used to fix potholes and unevenness in the road. They’re also trustworthy when it comes to repairing teeth that have been worn down due to usage or abuse, such as cracker nut munching, tooth grinding, and nail biting.

Your dentist will choose which type of filling is best suited to your particular condition or tooth concern. If fillings aren’t enough to keep your tooth, the dentist may recommend crowns, bridges, or veneers.

When is Filling Teeth Necessary?

When little cavities with moderate deterioration emerge, a dental filling is usually recommended. The most typical symptom if you require filling teeth would be a basic toothache, yet there are other symptoms to look for as well.

Some of the symptoms involve sensitivity to hot and cold temperatures, pain while biting or chewing, and a noticeable hole in your teeth that you can see or feel. Although amalgam fillings are often long-lasting, regular dental checkups are required to assure the procedure’s success.

What to Expect During the Dental Filling Process?

Cavities are a reasonably simple treatment for your dentist to perform. Your filling procedure session should last about an hour and this includes both your procedure and time spent at the office doing things like filling out paperwork.

To determine if a dental filling is the best treatment for you, your dentist will usually do an oral exam and take X-rays. A root canal may be required if the tooth decay is severe. During the decay removal, the dentist will numb your mouth with a local anaesthetic before drilling away any decay from the tooth.

The resin will then be coloured to match your natural teeth (depending on the material of fillings you’ve chosen).

Finally, after drilling out any leftover decay, the dentist will etch the tooth and fill the inside of the tooth with the filling. A UV light is utilised to immediately cure and solidify the filler. Your filling will be trimmed in any uneven areas, and it will be polished afterwards.

Dental Filling Price in Malaysia

Temporary fillings are the most cost effective choice, with prices ranging from RM80. These fillings are often constructed of a soft material that is intended to last for a limited time. Temporary fillings are frequently used as a stopgap measure before a permanent filling can be performed.

Amalgam fillings are manufactured from a combination of metals such as silver, mercury, tin, and copper. They are long-lasting and can be used for many years. The filling teeth price in Malaysia for amalgam fillings costs between RM80 and RM100.

A tooth-coloured resin is used to create composite or glass ionomer cement (GIC) fillings, which match the colour of the teeth. The teeth filling costs in Malaysia for this filling cost between RM120 and RM300, which is higher than amalgam fillings. In contrast to amalgam fillings, they have greater aesthetics and are less obvious.

However, filling teeth price in Malaysia varies based on the type of filling material used, the location of the dental facility, as well as the dental professional’s experience. Generally, the teeth filling cost in Malaysia is considered reasonable when compared to other countries.

It is usually advisable to consult with a dentist to obtain a precise estimate of costs.

Which is The Best Dental Filling Material in Malaysia?

Composite resin is the most used dental filling material in Malaysia, according to dental professionals. This is due to the fact that it is tooth-coloured, resilient, and easily adhered to the tooth surface. When compared to other materials such as amalgam, it also involves less destruction of healthy tooth structure.

How Long Will My Teeth Filling Last?

You know that fillings cannot last forever if you have ever had one fall out. The normal lifespan of a filling is 3 to 7 years, but you must undergo regular dental checkups to make sure it’s maintained well.

Contact your dentist right away if you notice any fading, chipping, or darkening behind the filling. These are frequent indications that the filling should be replaced due to further dental decay underneath the filling.
