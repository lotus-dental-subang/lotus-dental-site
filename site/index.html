Good to knowQuestions, answered

Questions, answered.

Where is Lotus Dental in Subang Jaya?

We are at No 8, Jalan SS15/4D, SS15, 47500 Subang Jaya, Selangor, with reserved parking for patients.

Do you treat both adults and children?

Yes. Lotus Dental is a family and kids-friendly clinic. We look after every age, from a child's first check-up to braces, implants and gum care for adults.

What are your opening hours?

Open daily, 9am to 6pm, including weekends.

How do I book?

Send us a WhatsApp on +60 16-211 9904 or call 03-5611 1772. We can often find you a slot the same day.

Do you work with insurance or medical cards?

We are a panel clinic for the providers shown below. Please bring your medical card to your visit. To check your panel before you come, just ask us on WhatsApp which panels we accept (no need to send a photo of your card).
