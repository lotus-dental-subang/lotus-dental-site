Wisdom Tooth Extraction Cost in Malaysia (2023)

By Lotus Dental Clinic, SS15 Subang Jaya · 26 June 2023

This guide was written in 2023. Prices may have changed. WhatsApp us for today’s price.

Wisdom tooth extractions are a form of dental surgery performed on teenagers and young adults. Wisdom teeth typically emerge several years after adult teeth and this can cause oral problems since wisdom teeth can crowd adult teeth and cause other troubles when there isn’t sufficient space. Affected wisdom teeth, which may not have enough space to develop normally, can cause infections beneath the gum line.

Some people may be prevented from getting the necessary treatment because of concerns about the cost of wisdom tooth removal. But, even with dental insurance, the typical cost of this operation can be less than you anticipate.

What is Wisdom Tooth Extraction?

The removal of one or more of the four wisdom teeth, often referred to as third molars, from the mouth is known as a wisdom tooth extraction. The wisdom teeth, which are the last to mature, typically erupt between the ages of 17 and 25.

If they do not develop normally or if there is not enough room in the mouth for them, they may occasionally result in issues like crowding, discomfort, infection, or damage to nearby teeth. To resolve the situation and preserve oral health, a dentist or oral surgeon may advise having the wisdom teeth extracted.

Average Cost of Wisdom Tooth Extraction in Malaysia

As you are probably aware, the majority of professional healthcare services are provided on a sliding scale of fees set by the government or the Dental Association rather than at a set price. The professionals often stick to a pricing range, and this range can be rather broad.

The wisdom teeth removal cost in Malaysia can differ based on aspects such as the intricacy of the extraction, the dentist’s level of experience, and the accessibility of the dental clinic.

The average cost per tooth might range from RM 500 to RM 2,500 (about $120 to $600).

It is advised for you to check with a dentist for a more precise figure based on your individual situation.

Are The Wisdom Teeth Removal Price Considered Affordable?

In comparison to certain countries, particularly those in the West, wisdom teeth removal prices in Malaysia might be considered to be fairly affordable.

Depending on the level of difficulty of the extraction, the location of the dental clinic, and the dentist’s skill, the wisdom tooth extraction cost in Malaysia can range from about RM 500 to RM 1,500 (about $120 to $360) for each tooth.

But it’s important to remember that a person’s perspective and financial circumstances can affect what they perceive as inexpensive or expensive.

Why Do I Need To Remove Impacted Wisdom Teeth?

You might need to have your wisdom teeth removed for the following typical reasons:

  • Other teeth are harmed.

Your other teeth may become crowded or crooked as a result of your wisdom teeth pressing against them as they erupt. Also, the teeth may be harmed as a result, which may make cleaning them more challenging.

  • Jaw ailment and cysts.

Wisdom teeth can develop fluid-filled sacs called dental cysts. They can harm the jawbone and nerves if left untreated.

  • Teeth and gums inflamed.

Around the base of the wisdom teeth, soft tissue might become entrapped and inflamed, which can result in an infection. Pericoronitis is the medical term for this, and it can be very painful.

​​What To Expect During A Wisdom Tooth Extraction?

The majority of the time, oral surgery facilities or dental clinics do wisdom tooth extractions. The patient will receive anesthesia to make them comfortable before having their wisdom teeth removed.

The actual extraction of the wisdom teeth entails removing the gum tissue surrounding them, pulling out the teeth, and closing up the extraction site with stitches. Depending on how complicated the wisdom teeth are, wisdom tooth removal usually takes an hour.

Patients might anticipate minor to moderate swelling, bruising, and soreness around their jawline after having their wisdom teeth removed. This is typical and will pass in a few days. To make sure that the location of the wisdom teeth extraction is healing properly, it’s crucial to adhere to any aftercare advice given by your dentist or oral surgeon.

Final Words

The extraction of wisdom teeth is a common dental treatment. It’s commonly recommended to safeguard your other teeth from issues down the road and to keep your mouth healthy. Most people bounce back fast and can go back to their normal routines in a few days.
