## Myobrace Malaysia

## What is Myobrace?

Myobrace is a set of transparent aligners that gradually realigns teeth. It is not only genetic factors that lead to [poor jaw growth](https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/diseases/22882-micrognathia#:~:text=Micrognathia%20is%20when%20your%20lower,itself%20as%20children%20grow%20older.) or teeth alignment; rather, poor oral habits picked up in early childhood may be the culprit. Myobrace for kids facilitates the release of organic growth and development. Children’s general oral health will be enhanced by this.

The Myobrace device is worn overnight and for a two-hour period during the day. This can solve orthodontic issues such as overbites, mouth breathing, and the misalignment of the jaw bones and teeth.

### Myobrace For Juniors

Myobrace for juniors is believed to be the most successful in primary dentition from the age of 3. It is a three-stage appliance system meant to stop habits such as thumb-sucking while correcting development issues.

### Myobrace For Kids

Myobrace for Kids is used for correcting habits which subsequently helps the natural development of the jaw. It works best when a child’s permanent front teeth have erupted. They are intended to be for children aged 5 to 10.

### Myobrace For Teens

Myobrace for Teens is a four-stage appliance system designed to help with permanent dentition development. As craniofacial growth has halted in this age group, the primary attention must be on arch development (particularly anterior arch development) and dental alignment to counteract the effects of years of poor habits.

This appliance line is intended to address these concerns while also addressing habit correction for improved orthodontic outcomes.

### Myobrace For Adults

Myobrace For Adults is a three-stage appliance system used in permanent dentition. Although the appliances are still quite successful at addressing respiratory and myofunctional abnormalities, the combination of little to no residual development and long-standing bad habits results in less effective orthodontic outcomes.

As a result, this appliance variety is almost usually used in conjunction with further arch development treatments and, on occasion, braces or clear aligners.

## The Myobrace Treatment Stages

-   **Stage 1 – Habit Correction**

The first stage of Myobrace treatment is Habit Correction, which includes teaching the patient to breathe through their nose rather than their mouth, retraining their tongue to rest in the proper position, swallowing properly, and keeping their lips together when not eating or speaking.

The child will have the best opportunity of growing their jaws to their proper and full size, allowing the teeth to come in straight and frequently preventing the need for braces later on by focusing on eliminating these oral habits that will cause crooked teeth and poor jaw growth.

-   **Stage 2 – Arch Development**

In addition to habit correction, further Arch Development might be necessary to broaden the upper jaw and create enough room for the teeth and tongue. Over 7-year-old children with undeveloped jaws may be advised to utilise The Myobrace System in conjunction with appliances. This is to promote further jaw development and expedite the healing process.

-   **Stage 3 – Dental Alignment**

The Myobrace will be used to Realign the teeth into their proper positions as the final permanent teeth begin to erupt. Compliance is crucial due to the nature of portable appliances.

The Myobrace appliances must be worn all day long and while you sleep. In order to get the best outcomes, patient cooperation and motivation are crucial. The use of braces may be necessary to achieve the child’s final alignment, depending on their specific needs and level of compliance.

-   **Stage 4 – Retention**

The goal of Retention, which is the last stage of Myobrace treatment, is to ensure that proper dental hygiene is maintained, typically avoiding the need to wear a permanent retainer or wire for a lengthy period of time.

Myobrace treatment enables more durable orthodontic outcomes and enhanced overall wellness with good compliance and the maintenance of the proper dental habits.

## Benefits of Myobrace

Myobrace has the following benefits:

-   **Teeth Straightening**

Myobrace is a device that can be used to address bad oral habits caused by a misaligned jaw or crooked teeth. The Myobrace works by applying minimal pressure to the teeth to straighten them. Patients who wear the Myobrace also do frequent exercises to improve their breathing patterns, muscle function, and tongue position, which helps to straighten their teeth.

-   **Teeth Damage is Reduced**

Braces and retainers aren’t for everyone, and in certain circumstances, they might be harmful. As an instance, older individuals who require orthodontic treatment are more vulnerable to orthodontic appliance deterioration. Myobrace, in general, reduces tooth damage because it is made of soft, silicone material that is gentle on delicate gums.

-   **Oral Hygiene That Can Be Maintained**

Since Myobrace is removable, it is simple to maintain perfect dental hygiene. When the Myobrace is removed in the morning and at night, teeth can be completely brushed. It is also easier to brush and floss after treatment when everything has been adjusted.

-   **Avoids Crooked Teeth**

Myobrace is useful not only for persons who have crooked teeth, but also for those who wish to avoid misalignment. Your orthodontic specialist may suggest Myobrace to treat any fundamental abnormalities that may be causing the appearance of crooked teeth.

-   **No Long-Term Retainers.**

Many individuals who have had braces removed find that their teeth have shifted back or become overcrowded. Myobrace enhances oral habits to ensure that once teeth are properly aligned, they remain straight.

-   **Comfort**

Myobrace’s delicate yet powerful method is at ease. Due to the mild pressure and duration of Myobrace treatment, any motions are not overly aggressive. This allows the mouth to spontaneously realign in the convenience of soft silicon material.
