## What Is A Root Canal Treatment?

Root canal treatment is intended to clean out the root canal that has become infected, preventing the tooth from becoming re-infected, and preserving the original tooth. A root canal involves removing the inflammatory or infected pulp and carefully cleaning, sanitising, filling, and sealing the inside of the tooth.

## What Takes Place During A Root Canal Treatment?

The interior section of the tooth is meticulously cleaned and shaped during the treatment, and the infected pulp is removed. The sterile space is then filled and sealed to stop the infection from returning and any discomfort.

To protect the endodontically treated tooth in the long run, crown restoration is advised. As a result, the number of visits needed for an endodontic treatment depends on the severity of the pulp infection and any necessary follow-up operations.

If your dentist or endodontist recommends a root canal treatment to treat a decayed or damaged tooth, you shouldn’t be worried. This method of care relieves discomfort and restores the health of millions of teeth each year.

There are numerous benefits to saving the natural tooth with root canal treatment:

-   Effective chewing
-   Normal biting pressure and feeling
-   A genuine appearance
-   Prevents undue wear or pressure on other teeth

## How Painful Are Root Canals?

A root canal isn’t more unpleasant than a routine dental operation like getting a filling or having a wisdom tooth extracted because patients are given anesthesia. A root canal, however, typically leaves the tooth a little uncomfortable or numb following the treatment and may even cause some minor discomfort for a couple of days.

## How Can You Tell Whether A Root Canal Treatment Is Necessary?

A deep cavity, a broken tooth caused by trauma or heredity, or problems from an old filling all call for root canal treatment. When patients realise that their teeth are highly sensitive, especially to heat and cold sensations, they typically require a root canal treatment.

There are a few signs that suggest a root canal treatment may be necessary:

-   Severe discomfort when biting or chewing
-   Gums with pimples
-   A tooth that is fractured or chipped
-   Persistence of heat or cold sensitivity even after the experience has passed
-   Enlarged or sensitive gums
-   Deep tooth decay or gum discolouration

## How Can I Get Ready For A Root Canal Treatment?

Your healthcare professional can address any inquiries you may have regarding the process before you start the treatment. You can do the following things to get ready for your root canal procedure:

-   **Take all prescribed medications as directed:** If there is a lot of infection present, you can be prescribed antibiotics or anti-inflammatory drugs a few days before your consultation.
-   **Avoid smoking:** Tobacco products hinder your body’s capacity to heal itself. A few days before your root canal appointment, try to cut out smoking completely if you can.
-   **Eat a nutritious meal:** Before your appointment because the local anesthesia used for root canal therapy will cause your mouth to be numb for a few hours.
